CULTURAL MEMORY LOCOMOTIVE

Welcome aboard a metaphorical journey through the evolution of media technologies. This interactive experience represents different media eras as interconnected train cars, each containing symbolic representations of its defining characteristics.

As you explore, you can engage with personified versions of these media epochs to discover their unique perspectives on knowledge, technology, society, memory, and change.

The locomotive metaphor represents how media technologies build upon each other while maintaining forward momentum through history.

Print Media Era
The Gutenberg revolution - mechanized reproduction of text, birth of mass literacy, standardization of knowledge.
Radio Wave Era
Invisible transmission across distances - the first electronic mass medium, creating shared temporal experiences.
Television Era
Moving images in living rooms - visual storytelling meets mass distribution, the electronic hearth of culture.
Internet Era
Networked consciousness - bidirectional flows, hypertext, and the dissolution of traditional media boundaries.
